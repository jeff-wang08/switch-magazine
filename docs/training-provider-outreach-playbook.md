# Training Provider Outreach Playbook

## What I Should Have Set Up First

You need the full sales machine, not just a list. The minimum system is:

1. A dedicated sender identity.
2. A clean contact sheet.
3. A tight offer.
4. A 7-10 day email/call cadence.
5. A simple CRM status tracker.
6. A qualification script that quickly finds out whether they buy leads.

## Sender Setup

Use a separate outbound domain or subdomain so your main domain is protected.

Recommended setup:

- Domain: `get[brand].com`, `try[brand].com`, or `partners.[yourdomain].com`
- Inbox: `partners@...` or `jef@...`
- Display name: your real name, not a generic brand
- Email provider: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
- Required DNS: SPF, DKIM, DMARC
- DMARC starting policy: `p=none`
- Signature: name, company, phone, website or LinkedIn

Do not blast from a brand-new inbox. Warm it up gradually:

- Days 1-3: 5-10 manual emails/day
- Days 4-7: 10-20/day
- Week 2: 20-40/day if replies and deliverability look healthy

## Offer

Lead with a small test, not a big agency pitch.

Positioning:

> We help California-approved career training providers connect with adults interested in online or hybrid programs in IT, cybersecurity, data, bookkeeping, accounting, and business administration.

Ask:

> Do you currently work with outside partners for qualified student inquiries or booked admissions calls?

Do you currently work with any outside lead, referral, or booked-appointment partners?

## Qualification Questions

Ask these before discussing pricing:

- Which programs are you actively trying to enroll students into?
- Do you accept online or hybrid students from across California?
- What makes a prospect qualified for your admissions team?
- Do you currently buy leads, referrals, appointments, or agency traffic?
- Do you prefer raw inquiries, booked calls, or applications?
- Are WIOA/ETPL-funded students valuable to you?
- Who approves a small pilot?

## CRM Statuses

Use these statuses:

- Not contacted
- Email sent
- Called
- Replied
- Wrong contact
- Interested
- Needs follow-up
- Pilot proposed
- Not a fit
- Closed

## Pricing Test

Do not lead with pricing. If they ask, give ranges and ask what model they already use.

Good starting models:

- Qualified inquiry: low-friction test
- Booked admissions call: better if they distrust raw leads
- Enrolled student: only after trust and tracking are in place

Best opening line:

> We usually start with a small pilot so both sides can judge lead quality before committing to a larger volume or pricing model.
