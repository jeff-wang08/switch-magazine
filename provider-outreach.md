# Switch Magazine Provider Outreach

## Positioning

Switch Magazine routes career-minded California applicants to independent training providers in practical, job-aligned categories. The first commercial motion is a hybrid prepaid pilot: start with a low-risk quality conversation, define what counts as qualified, then sell a small prepaid pilot credit/package for qualified lead delivery.

Do not promise enrollment, admission, funding, completion, certification, or job placement. The offer is qualified applicant routing.

## First Email

Subject: California applicants for [program category]

Hi [Name],

I’m building Switch Magazine, a small applicant-matching channel for California adults looking at practical career training.

Your [program category] program looks like a potential fit because we are focusing on online-friendly / hybrid tracks where adults can compare options without guessing where to start.

Are you open to a small pilot for qualified California applicants in [program category]?

The first step would be simple: you tell me what counts as a qualified applicant for your admissions team, then I route a small batch that matches that profile. If the quality is useful, we can structure it as a small prepaid pilot credit/package rather than a long contract.

Best,
Jeff

## Call Opener

“I’m building a small applicant routing channel for California adults comparing practical training programs. I’m not asking you to commit to a long campaign today. I want to understand what your admissions team considers a qualified applicant, what programs you want more demand for, and whether a small prepaid pilot could make sense if the lead quality is there.”

## Qualification Questions

- Which programs are you most interested in filling right now?
- What makes someone qualified enough for your admissions team to contact?
- Are there locations, start timelines, education levels, or funding situations that are a bad fit?
- Do you prefer online, hybrid, or local applicants?
- Who should receive qualified applicant details if we run a small pilot?
- What would make the first 5 to 10 applicants feel successful?

## Objection Replies

### “We already have marketing.”

Totally. I’m not trying to replace your current channels. The pilot is meant to test whether a small stream of matched California applicants can add incremental conversations for programs you already want to fill.

### “We only pay on enrollment.”

I understand why that is attractive. For this first version, I’m trying to keep the pilot tied to applicant quality because enrollment depends on admissions, pricing, funding, and internal follow-up after the introduction. We can define strict qualified-lead criteria before any prepaid pilot.

### “Send leads first and then we’ll decide.”

I’m happy to start with a fit conversation and define the criteria first. If there is alignment, the pilot works best as a small prepaid credit/package so I can prioritize sourcing and routing for your program without pretending this is a broad free trial.

### “What do you mean by qualified?”

Qualified means whatever we agree is worth your admissions team’s time before the pilot starts. For example: California location, target program interest, preferred format, start timeline, funding need, education level, and consent to be contacted.

### “Can you guarantee enrollments?”

No. Switch Magazine is not a school and does not control admission, funding, completion, certification, or job outcomes. The pilot is for qualified applicant routing, not guaranteed enrollment.
